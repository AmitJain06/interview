Top-K Elements

Continuously surface the K highest-ranked items from a large stream.

Design a service that tracks millions of events per minute (searches, plays, votes) and answers "what are the current top-K items?" with low latency.

  1. Count frequencies with hash counters, or approximate them with a Count-Min Sketch when the keyspace is huge.
  2. Maintain a min-heap of size K — push new candidates and pop the smallest to keep the heap at K elements.
  3. Use bucket sort when counts are bounded, giving near O(n) selection instead of O(n log k).
  4. At scale, compute per-shard top-K sets and merge them at a coordinator node (map-reduce style).
  5. Cache hot result sets with a short TTL and serve slightly stale data while recomputing.
